Karaoke Maker FAQ

Why can I still hear faint vocals?

Reverb and instruments that share vocal frequencies can remain in the backing stem. This is common in AI separation.

Is this intended for public karaoke publishing?

No rights are granted by this tool. Obtain appropriate rights before distributing or monetizing a backing track.

Can I cut out a verse for repeat practice?

Yes. The browser-based Audio Cutter can export a selected section as WAV without uploading the file again.
