Free AI Vocal Remover for Music You Can Use

CleanStems is an AI-powered vocal remover and stem splitter designed for creators who need quick, practical audio separation. You can use it to test vocal isolation, create a draft instrumental, extract an acapella reference, or explore how a song is arranged into stems.

What can CleanStems separate?

Depending on the audio and model output, CleanStems can help separate vocals, instrumentals, drums, bass, and other musical elements. AI stem separation is not perfect, so complex mixes may include artifacts, bleed, or reduced quality.

Who is it for?

  • Singers who need practice instrumentals from permitted tracks.
  • DJs testing remix or mashup ideas.
  • Music teachers preparing learning materials.
  • Producers studying arrangements or cleaning demo stems.
  • Video creators working with audio they own or license.
