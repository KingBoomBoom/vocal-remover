DMCA Policy

CleanStems responds to valid copyright notices. If you believe your copyrighted work is being infringed through our service, please email support@cleanstems.com with the information below.

Notice Requirements

  • Your full legal name and contact information.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • The URL or identifying information for the allegedly infringing material or activity.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Repeat Infringers

CleanStems may restrict or terminate access for users who repeatedly violate copyright or misuse the service.
