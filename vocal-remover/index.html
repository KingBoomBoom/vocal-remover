Vocal Remover FAQ

Does vocal removal work perfectly on every track?

No. Dense arrangements, heavy reverb and vocals that overlap with lead instruments can leave audible artifacts.

Can I use a separated instrumental commercially?

Not automatically. Separating audio does not provide copyright permissions or a commercial license.

What should I do with a practice instrumental?

Open the instrumental result, then use our Audio Cutter to trim the verse or chorus you want to repeat.
