Which Tool Should I Choose?

Use Vocal Remover if you want two broad outputs: vocals and instrumental. Use Karaoke Maker when your objective is a singing practice track. Use Audio Cutter for rehearsing a verse or chorus without sending a file away from your browser, and BPM Tapper when you only need a quick tempo estimate.
