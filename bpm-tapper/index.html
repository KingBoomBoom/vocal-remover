How to Tap BPM Accurately

Start on a strong beat.

Kick drums or clear downbeats are easier to follow than vocals or fills.

Tap steadily for eight beats.

More even taps create a more stable BPM estimate than two quick clicks.

Reset when the section changes.

Breakdowns and tempo shifts can change the average; start fresh for a new section.

Use BPM With Your Practice Track

After producing a permitted backing stem in Karaoke Maker, tap its tempo to note your rehearsal speed. If you need only a loop, use Audio Cutter to export the useful section.
