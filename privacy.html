Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

CleanStems provides AI vocal removal and stem separation tools. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the site.

Audio Files

During beta, audio processing may be handled by a shared cloud tool embedded on CleanStems. Files may be transmitted to that processing environment to generate stems. Do not upload sensitive, private, or copyrighted audio unless you have permission to process it.

Technical Data

We may receive basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and diagnostic logs to keep the service secure and reliable.

Future Accounts and Payments

If CleanStems adds accounts, credits, or paid plans, we may process account and payment-related information through trusted third-party providers. Payment card details should be handled by the payment provider, not directly by CleanStems.

Cookies and Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics and advertising tools to understand site performance and support the free tier. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@cleanstems.com.
